Your tiny daily ritual

Five soft things, just for you

Tick them off as you go today. They're small on purpose — solo living is built one gentle, doable moment at a time. 🌼

  • Make your bed & open a window
  • Cook (or plate) one real meal for yourself
  • Move your body for 10 minutes
  • Text one person you love
  • Do one thing purely because it's nice
0 of 5 done — every little bit counts 🌷
